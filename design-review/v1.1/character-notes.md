# v1.1 character study: not approved final artwork

The reference sheets are source artwork by Capcom, reproduced in this private
review pack as attributed reference only. They are not application assets.

## Original woman concept

`woman-concept.svg` is a new ink-and-colour costume rough, not a finished
painted game character. Deliberate features: broad shoulder cape, structured
teal coat over an ochre pleated skirt, silver-streaked swept hair, a long face,
a ledger and quill, restrained jewellery, strong vertical posture. Her native
canvas and intended seated scale equal the two other judicial characters.

The layered costume, modelling of the face, and fine fold lines are the design
proposal. The reference sheet's painterly fidelity has NOT been achieved by
this vector rough. No claim of approval or art-quality parity is made.

## Japanese-inspired original, production brief

Use an angular shoulder line, high indigo collar, layered long robe, narrow
ivory facings, cropped silver hair, and a short, squared beard. A closed folio
and one open hand provide an alternative to the reference's oversized gavel.
Do not copy its hat, face, floral chest embroidery, beard contour, or costume.

## British-inspired original, production brief

Use a substantial bell-shaped wine robe over a charcoal waistcoat, a pale
shoulder cape, narrow spectacles, swept-back grey hair, and a long clean-shaven
chin. A rolled opinion and a small desk bell replace the reference props.
Do not copy its ringlet beard, teal waist sash, red neck bow, or robe pattern.

## Roles and names

No invented names. Fairness, Feasibility, and Cost are the only proposed public
labels. Which artwork receives which perspective remains UNASSIGNED. The
composition study labels the bench as a collective panel, not individual roles.

## Hard production gate

The final three portraits must be produced together and reviewed together at
full native resolution and at 1440 × 900 stage scale before animation work.
The composition study uses clearly marked figure stand-ins for the two male
characters. Those stand-ins must not migrate into the delivered application.
